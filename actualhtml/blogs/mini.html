Ever since being introduced to it's music around a year and change ago, i've been bumping Vylet Pony's music everywhere and recommending her to friends. But, i haven't made a recommendation album yet, and its because i feel i can't do it justice yet.

If you want my opinion, Monarch of Monsters is my absolute fave, followed closely by Love and Ponystep, Girls who are Wizards, Carousel, Gonarch's Lair, Cutiemarks, Queen of Misfits, and Can opener's Notebook. They're all really good for different reasons (VP's Range is so impressive when it comes to genre and production) but there's a LOT of lore when it comes to VP!

With each album i hear and recognize nods to other songs from different albums, and more insights to both the characters and VP. Don't even get me started with the art books and novella i realized recently when I actually bought the albums -- the rabbit hole is deep, and there's a ton of songs i wanna listen to with the lyrics side by side.

For now though, i almost feel like i would be doing the recommendation an injustice to make some without going into a full dive and understanding the pieces. Will I make one at one point? Hell yeah. But for now just bump this pony music i Promise you will find a new artist to keep your eye on and ear open to.
